My school had a 3d printing competition on who can design the best storage box for storing student candidates' exam cards in the best and most convenient way.
<br /><br />
It took a couple revisions of the design to get to the finished one pictured, and there are many little details I made to make it more convenient to store and take out cards.
<br /><br />
In addition, the 2 containers allow to store groups of cards together (and label the group too) as well as making it easy to take them out and give out.
